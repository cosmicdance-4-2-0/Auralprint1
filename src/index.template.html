Camera

Perform through the view without changing the world.

Camera mode off

Camera mode works while this panel is hidden. Focus the canvas to use movement keys.

Pan along the current view's axes. Positive rotation turns the camera counterclockwise; the scene appears clockwise. The simulation remains independent of the view.

Reset Camera (C) restores the factory view. Fit View (F) fits the world, retained trails and potential Orb/Ring extent once, preserving rotation. Zoom limits may prevent a complete fit.

Camera movement changes only your live view. Save it explicitly to update the Scene view used by Copy Share Link. Restore returns to that saved view. Different screen shapes may show more or less of the world.

Speeds apply to held movement keys. Live movement, mode and speeds last for this session. Only the explicitly saved Scene view is included in presets.

Control reference

Camera mode + canvas focus: W/S move forward/back; A/D strafe; Q/E rotate counterclockwise/clockwise. Hold Shift to boost movement and rotation. Combine keys to move and turn.

Drag to grab the world; wheel to zoom at the pointer. Arrows pan in steps; +/− zoom; [/] rotate clockwise/counterclockwise; C or Home resets; F fits the world and composition; Escape exits camera mode.

H hides/restores panels; Space pauses visual motion; R resets visuals; N/P change tracks. Panel buttons also work with camera mode off.

Simulation World

World dimensions set the reference size for Orb and Ring proportions. Points and particles can exist beyond these dimensions. The default view fits the shorter world dimension to the shorter viewport dimension. Changing world dimensions during a session changes Orb and Ring geometry. Existing particles remain at their original world positions, so trails may appear discontinuous.
